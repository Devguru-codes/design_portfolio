About me

I am a final-year student at IIIT Nagpur and a Product Manager. I work at the intersection of systems engineering, data and user experience.

My approach to product is deliberately analytical. I start by understanding the core user journey, find the friction with data, and then build — whether that means redesigning a checkout flow for conversion or architecting a knowledge graph that refuses to store a fact it cannot prove. The goal is always measurable impact, and an honest account of what was and wasn't measured.
